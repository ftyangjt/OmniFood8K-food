# 答辩可能问题与细节 50 问

本文档整理老师可能询问的问题，每个问题包含中文和英文，便于汇报准备。

## 1

问：这个项目解决什么问题？  
Q: What problem does this project solve?

答：本项目从单张食物图片估计热量、质量、脂肪、碳水和蛋白质。  
A: It estimates calories, mass, fat, carbohydrate, and protein from a single food image.

## 2

问：这是分类任务还是回归任务？  
Q: Is this a classification task or a regression task?

答：这是回归任务，因为输出是连续的营养数值。  
A: It is a regression task because the outputs are continuous nutrition values.

## 3

问：模型输入是什么？  
Q: What are the model inputs?

答：输入包括 RGB 食物图片和对应的伪深度图 `rgb-d.png`。  
A: The inputs are an RGB food image and its pseudo-depth image `rgb-d.png`.

## 4

问：模型输出是什么？  
Q: What are the model outputs?

答：输出为 `calories_kcal, mass_g, fat_g, carb_g, protein_g`。  
A: The outputs are `calories_kcal, mass_g, fat_g, carb_g, protein_g`.

## 5

问：为什么使用深度图？  
Q: Why do we use depth images?

答：深度图提供形状、厚度和份量信息，有助于估计质量和热量。  
A: Depth images provide shape, thickness, and portion cues, which help estimate mass and calories.

## 6

问：深度图怎么生成？  
Q: How are depth images generated?

答：使用 Depth Anything V2 从 RGB 图像生成伪深度图。  
A: Pseudo-depth images are generated from RGB images using Depth Anything V2.

## 7

问：数据集如何划分？  
Q: How is the dataset split?

答：训练集由 `train_new333.txt` 决定，测试集由 `test_new333.txt` 决定。  
A: The training set is defined by `train_new333.txt`, and the test set is defined by `test_new333.txt`.

## 8

问：标签文件每行包含什么？  
Q: What does each label line contain?

答：每行包含 `sample_id mass calories protein fat carb`。  
A: Each line contains `sample_id mass calories protein fat carb`.

## 9

问：当前输入分辨率是多少？  
Q: What is the current input resolution?

答：当前统一使用 `320 x 320`。  
A: The current input resolution is `320 x 320`.

## 10

问：为什么把分辨率改成 320？  
Q: Why was the resolution changed to 320?

答：为了减少计算量和显存占用，加快训练和推理。  
A: It reduces computation and GPU memory usage, making training and inference faster.

## 11

问：降低分辨率有什么代价？  
Q: What is the cost of reducing resolution?

答：可能损失纹理、边缘和小配菜等细节，精度可能下降。  
A: It may lose texture, edge, and small ingredient details, which can reduce accuracy.

## 12

问：RGB 分支使用什么网络？  
Q: What network is used for the RGB branch?

答：当前使用 Swin-T 风格的 Swin Transformer。  
A: The current RGB branch uses a Swin-T style Swin Transformer.

## 13

问：Swin-T 是什么？  
Q: What is Swin-T?

答：Swin-T 是 Swin Transformer Tiny，是较轻量的视觉 Transformer。  
A: Swin-T is Swin Transformer Tiny, a lightweight vision Transformer.

## 14

问：为什么不用 Swin-B？  
Q: Why not use Swin-B?

答：Swin-B 更大更慢，当前项目为了适应本地训练进行了轻量化。  
A: Swin-B is larger and slower; the current project uses a lightweight design for local training.

## 15

问：深度分支使用什么网络？  
Q: What network is used for the depth branch?

答：使用 DepthAdapterV4 和 ConvNeXt-Tiny。  
A: It uses DepthAdapterV4 and ConvNeXt-Tiny.

## 16

问：ConvNeXt-Tiny 是什么？  
Q: What is ConvNeXt-Tiny?

答：它是 ConvNeXt 家族中的轻量版本，用于提取图像特征。  
A: It is a lightweight member of the ConvNeXt family for image feature extraction.

## 17

问：ConvNeXt-Tiny 和 ConvNeXt-Small 有什么区别？  
Q: What is the difference between ConvNeXt-Tiny and ConvNeXt-Small?

答：Tiny 的层数更少，速度更快；Small 更深，表达能力更强。  
A: Tiny has fewer layers and is faster; Small is deeper and more expressive.

## 18

问：DepthAdapterV4 的作用是什么？  
Q: What is the role of DepthAdapterV4?

答：它将深度图适配到更适合 ConvNeXt 处理的特征空间。  
A: It adapts depth images into a feature space more suitable for ConvNeXt.

## 19

问：融合模块是什么？  
Q: What is the fusion module?

答：融合模块是 `FusionNet_3Branch_UNet_FFT`。  
A: The fusion module is `FusionNet_3Branch_UNet_FFT`.

## 20

问：为什么使用频域融合？  
Q: Why use frequency-domain fusion?

答：频域融合可以同时利用高频细节和低频整体结构。  
A: Frequency-domain fusion uses both high-frequency details and low-frequency global structure.

## 21

问：高频信息代表什么？  
Q: What does high-frequency information represent?

答：高频通常表示边缘、纹理和局部细节。  
A: High-frequency information usually represents edges, textures, and local details.

## 22

问：低频信息代表什么？  
Q: What does low-frequency information represent?

答：低频通常表示整体轮廓、形状和大尺度结构。  
A: Low-frequency information usually represents global contours, shapes, and large-scale structure.

## 23

问：预测头是什么？  
Q: What is a prediction head?

答：预测头是把融合特征转换成最终营养数值的小网络。  
A: A prediction head maps fused features to final nutrition values.

## 24

问：为什么从 5 个独立预测头改为共享预测头？  
Q: Why replace five independent heads with a shared head?

答：共享头减少重复计算，并让相关营养指标共享特征。  
A: A shared head reduces duplicate computation and lets related nutrition targets share features.

## 25

问：为什么输出要使用 Softplus？  
Q: Why use Softplus for outputs?

答：营养值不能为负，Softplus 可以保证输出非负。  
A: Nutrition values cannot be negative, and Softplus ensures non-negative outputs.

## 26

问：batch size 是什么？  
Q: What is batch size?

答：batch size 是一次送入模型训练或推理的样本数量。  
A: Batch size is the number of samples processed at one training or inference step.

## 27

问：epoch 是什么？  
Q: What is an epoch?

答：模型完整遍历一遍训练集称为一个 epoch。  
A: One epoch means the model has gone through the entire training set once.

## 28

问：checkpoint 是什么？  
Q: What is a checkpoint?

答：checkpoint 是训练过程中保存的模型状态文件。  
A: A checkpoint is a saved model state during training.

## 29

问：`ckpt_best.pth` 表示什么？  
Q: What does `ckpt_best.pth` mean?

答：它表示验证指标最好的模型权重。  
A: It stores the model weights with the best validation performance.

## 30

问：为什么旧 checkpoint 不能直接用？  
Q: Why cannot old checkpoints be used directly?

答：因为模型结构已从旧的 5 头版本改成了轻量共享头版本。  
A: Because the architecture changed from the old five-head model to the lightweight shared-head model.

## 31

问：训练时为什么可以 partial load？  
Q: Why allow partial loading during training?

答：训练可以利用可匹配的预训练参数作为初始化。  
A: Training can use matching pretrained tensors as initialization.

## 32

问：推理时为什么要严格加载 checkpoint？  
Q: Why use strict checkpoint loading during inference?

答：推理结果必须可信，不能悄悄跳过不匹配参数。  
A: Inference results must be reliable, so mismatched parameters should not be silently skipped.

## 33

问：MAE 是什么？  
Q: What is MAE?

答：MAE 是平均绝对误差，表示预测值和真实值的平均差距。  
A: MAE is mean absolute error, the average absolute difference between predictions and labels.

## 34

问：PMAE 是什么？  
Q: What is PMAE?

答：PMAE 是相对误差，用误差除以真实值总量。  
A: PMAE is a relative error, calculated as error divided by the total ground-truth value.

## 35

问：为什么热量单位使用 kcal？  
Q: Why use kcal for calories?

答：营养学中常用千卡表示食物热量，公式也对应 kcal。  
A: Food energy is commonly represented in kilocalories, and the formula is based on kcal.

## 36

问：热量一致性约束是什么？  
Q: What is the calorie consistency constraint?

答：它约束 `calories ≈ 9 * fat + 4 * carb + 4 * protein`。  
A: It constrains `calories ≈ 9 * fat + 4 * carb + 4 * protein`.

## 37

问：为什么加入热量一致性约束？  
Q: Why add the calorie consistency constraint?

答：它引入营养学先验，减少不合理预测。  
A: It introduces nutrition prior knowledge and reduces unreasonable predictions.

## 38

问：模态对齐损失是什么？  
Q: What is the modal alignment loss?

答：它鼓励 RGB 特征和深度特征在表示空间中对齐。  
A: It encourages RGB features and depth features to align in representation space.

## 39

问：为什么要做 RGB/Depth 对齐？  
Q: Why align RGB and Depth features?

答：因为两种模态描述同一食物，对齐后融合更稳定。  
A: Because both modalities describe the same food, alignment makes fusion more stable.

## 40

问：推理脚本做了哪些稳定性优化？  
Q: What stability improvements were made in inference?

答：包括深度归一化防除 0、严格加载 checkpoint、CSV 规范写入。  
A: They include safe depth normalization, strict checkpoint loading, and robust CSV writing.

## 41

问：为什么 CSV 使用 `csv.writer` 或 `DictWriter`？  
Q: Why use `csv.writer` or `DictWriter`?

答：它比手动拼字符串更安全，能处理特殊文件名。  
A: It is safer than manual string concatenation and handles special filenames.

## 42

问：为什么 CSV 要保存 image_path 和 depth_path？  
Q: Why save image_path and depth_path in CSV?

答：这样后续分析和可视化可以追踪每个结果来源。  
A: It allows later analysis and visualization to trace each prediction source.

## 43

问：为什么文件夹推理要排序？  
Q: Why sort files during folder inference?

答：排序可以保证每次推理输出顺序稳定。  
A: Sorting keeps the output order stable across runs.

## 44

问：batch 推理有什么作用？  
Q: What is the purpose of batch inference?

答：批量送入营养模型可以提高文件夹推理速度。  
A: Sending multiple images to the nutrition model improves folder inference speed.

## 45

问：batch 推理有什么代价？  
Q: What is the cost of batch inference?

答：batch 越大显存占用越高，显存不足时需要调小。  
A: Larger batches use more GPU memory, so the batch size should be reduced if memory is insufficient.

## 46

问：为什么 Depth Anything 仍然逐张生成深度图？  
Q: Why does Depth Anything still generate depth one by one?

答：当前脚本优先保证稳定和兼容，后续可以继续做深度模型 batch 化。  
A: The current script prioritizes stability and compatibility; depth batching can be a future improvement.

## 47

问：当前轻量化的收益是什么？  
Q: What are the benefits of the lightweight design?

答：训练更快、显存更低，更适合本地设备运行。  
A: It trains faster, uses less GPU memory, and is more suitable for local machines.

## 48

问：当前轻量化的代价是什么？  
Q: What is the cost of the lightweight design?

答：模型表达能力可能下降，极细节信息可能不足。  
A: Model capacity may decrease, and fine details may be less well captured.

## 49

问：如果想提高性能可以怎么做？  
Q: How can performance be improved?

答：可以提高输入分辨率、增强深度分支、改进融合模块或加入分割信息。  
A: We can increase input resolution, strengthen the depth branch, improve fusion, or add segmentation information.

## 50

问：项目最终从数据到结果的完整路径是什么？  
Q: What is the complete path from data to result?

答：读取 RGB 和深度图，预处理，双分支提特征，融合，预测 5 个营养值，输出 CSV。  
A: It reads RGB and depth images, preprocesses them, extracts dual-branch features, fuses them, predicts five nutrition values, and writes CSV output.
